The lotus is a beautiful flower that grows in ponds and lakes. It usually has pink or white petals and green leaves. The lotus is the national flower of India. It symbolizes purity, peace, and beauty. The lotus teaches us to stay positive and overcome difficulties.

The sunflower is a bright and beautiful flower with yellow petals. It looks like the shining sun and brings happiness. Sunflowers are commonly found in gardens and fields. Their seeds are used to produce oil and provide food. The sunflower teaches us to stay happy and positive.
